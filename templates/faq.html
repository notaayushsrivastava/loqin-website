Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Loqin

Got questions about how Loqin manages Wi-Fi logins, stores passwords, or handles background connectivity? We have answers.

What is Loqin?

Loqin is a lightweight desktop system tray client for Windows and macOS. It continuously monitors your hostel Wi-Fi connection and automatically re-authenticates with the campus captive portal whenever your login session drops or times out.

Is Loqin safe? Where are my credentials stored?

Loqin is 100% open source and licensed under GNU GPLv3. It does not use insecure plaintext configuration files. On Windows, credentials are stored in the Windows Credential Manager. On macOS, credentials are protected by the Apple Keychain. Credentials never leave your machine except when transmitted directly to the campus captive portal gateway during authentication.

Does Loqin track me or collect analytics?

No. Loqin contains zero tracking libraries, telemetrySDKs, analytics beacons, or remote logging. The app only sends requests to the local network captive portal gateway. You can inspect the entire Python codebase on our GitHub repository.

Is Loqin affiliated with Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)?

No. Loqin is an independent community project created by a student developer to help fellow students avoid having to repeatedly open browser tabs to re-authenticate with hostel Wi-Fi. It is not endorsed, operated, or officially affiliated with VIT.

Does Loqin support macOS?

Yes! Loqin offers a dedicated macOS build distributed as a .dmg installer. It runs smoothly on both Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) and Intel Macs running macOS 11 (Big Sur) through macOS Sequoia and newer.

Why is there no mobile or Android app?

Modern mobile operating systems (especially Android 12+ and iOS) aggressively kill long-running background tasks and battery-saving foreground services to preserve mobile battery life, making persistent captive portal clients unreliable. Loqin is currently focused on providing the best possible background experience on desktop laptops.

Does running Loqin drain my laptop battery?

No. Loqin is designed with efficiency as a primary priority. It spends over 99% of its runtime suspended, waking up for milliseconds to test network connectivity. Memory usage is typically under 30MB, and CPU consumption while idling is essentially 0%.

How do I pause Loqin when using my mobile hotspot?

When you are off-campus or tethering to a mobile phone hotspot, right-click the Loqin icon in your Windows notification tray (or click the menu bar item on Mac) and select Pause. When you reconnect to VIT hostel Wi-Fi, simply select Resume or Connect Now.

How do I uninstall Loqin?

On Windows, go to Windows Settings → Apps → Installed Apps and click Uninstall on Loqin. On macOS, simply quit the app from your menu bar and drag Loqin from your Applications folder to the Trash.
