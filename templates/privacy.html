Loqin PC

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Loqin PC is designed to automate sign-in to VIT hostel Wi-Fi. This policy explains how the website and application handle information.

Information the application uses

To authenticate you with your Wi-Fi provider, Loqin PC may use the credentials you enter during setup. These credentials are used only to perform Wi-Fi authentication on your device. The application does not transmit them to the Loqin developer or to any independent analytics service.

Website information

This website does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information. Links to GitHub, installer downloads, and other third-party services are governed by those services’ own privacy policies. Standard server or hosting logs may record technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request time for security and operational purposes.

Data sharing

Loqin does not sell, rent, or share personal information. Information needed for Wi-Fi authentication is sent to the relevant Wi-Fi login service as required to provide the feature.

Security

Please keep your device secure and use a strong device login. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website or application changes. Questions may be raised through the project’s GitHub issue tracker.
